# Credits

All game code, design, creature designs (Znomes), and names are original
to Znome Kop.

## Art

World tiles, objects and structures are original, generated by
`tools/art_tiles.py`.

Player and NPC sprites are derived from the following asset pack,
converted to 1-bit for the Playdate by `tools/convert_packs.py`. Only
converted 1-bit derivatives (`tools/pack_art/`) are included in this
repository; the original pack is not redistributed.

- **GB Village** by Hoyb — https://hoyb.itch.io/free-gb-village
  Player and NPC sprites.
  License (itch.io page): free for personal and commercial projects,
  may be modified and edited; the asset pack itself may not be resold.

## Znome generation

Znome battle sprites are generated by `spritegen/`, a standalone
reusable package containing a 1-bit port
of the sprite-generation algorithm from **SpriteGenerator** by Deep-Fold
(MIT License) — https://github.com/Deep-Fold/SpriteGenerator /
https://deep-fold.itch.io/pixel-sprite-generator
