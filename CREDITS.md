# Credits

All game code, design, creature designs (Znomes), and names are original
to Znome Kop.

## Art

World, building, player and NPC art is derived from the following asset
packs, converted to 1-bit for the Playdate by `tools/convert_packs.py`.
Only converted 1-bit derivatives (`tools/pack_art/`) are included in this
repository; the original packs are not redistributed.

- **GB Village** by Hoyb — https://hoyb.itch.io/free-gb-village
  Terrain, buildings, fences, player and NPC sprites.
  License (itch.io page): free for personal and commercial projects,
  may be modified and edited; the asset pack itself may not be resold.

- **Game Boy Top-down RPG Fantasy Tileset (FREE)** by Gumpy Function —
  https://gumpyfunction.itch.io/game-boy-rpg-fantasy-tileset-free
  Reference/support tiles.
  License (itch.io page): free for non-commercial and commercial projects
  (excluding NFT/blockchain), may be edited; packs may not be
  redistributed. Suggested credit: "Background Assets by Gumpy Function
  (gumpyfunction.itch.io)".

- **GB Studio: SciFi Tiles** by The Pixel Nook —
  https://the-pixel-nook.itch.io/gb-studio-scifi-tiles
  Sci-fi terrain, props and structures.
  NOTE: no explicit license text on the itch.io page. Confirm terms with
  the author before a commercial release.

## Znome generation

Znome battle sprites are generated by `spritegen/`, a standalone
reusable package containing a 1-bit port
of the sprite-generation algorithm from **SpriteGenerator** by Deep-Fold
(MIT License) — https://github.com/Deep-Fold/SpriteGenerator /
https://deep-fold.itch.io/pixel-sprite-generator
