# Credits

All game code, design, creature designs (Znomes), and names are original
to Znome Kop.

## Art

Player/NPC sprites and scene backdrops are original AI-generated art
(raw generations in `tools/ai_raw/`, produced with a frontier image
model per `.agents/skills/ai-art/SKILL.md`), converted for the Playdate
by `tools/ai_convert.py`. AI outputs are owned by the project and are
fine for commercial use.

Legacy world tiles, objects and structures are original, generated by
`tools/art_tiles.py`.

## Znome generation

Znome battle sprites are generated by `spritegen/`, a standalone
reusable package containing a 1-bit port
of the sprite-generation algorithm from **SpriteGenerator** by Deep-Fold
(MIT License) — https://github.com/Deep-Fold/SpriteGenerator /
https://deep-fold.itch.io/pixel-sprite-generator
